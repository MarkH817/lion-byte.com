title: Whimsy-Maxxing
description: Choose to feel joy and experience whimsy where you can.
---

I want to know what brings you joy. I want you to enjoy what you make and learn a bit about you through that experience. Choose to be authentically yourself. Choose to love things openly. Others who care will connect over time.

But more importantly, choose whimsy and joy for yourself. It's so easy to forget to do this, but it's worth the experience. I want you to eventually look back and know that those moments of joy brought you warmth and comfort.
